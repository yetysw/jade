# Manage conversations

At the end of this page you can organise, search, export and forget your conversations.

- **Folders and pins.** Drag a conversation to a folder, to Pinned or back to Conversations. Right click a conversation or a folder to rename, move or delete it.
- **Search.** The search field looks into the content of your conversations.
- **Titles** are generated by the model.
- **Export.** Export a conversation to a Word file in `Documents\Jade`. It opens if Word is installed.
- **Memory.** jade keeps facts across conversations, locally, in `memory.db` in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Jade`. You can list, delete or clear them. It ignores sentences that jade said about itself.
- **Incognito.** In incognito mode the history is not saved.
- **Delete everything.** The settings can delete the whole history.
- **Give feedback.** The button builds a diagnostic zip (logs, version, state; no conversation content, no excerpt, no secret), copies it to the clipboard, and opens your mail client with a message to `FEEDBACK_EMAIL`. If the copy fails, the zip is shown in Explorer. Attach it yourself: a mail link cannot carry an attachment.

Conversations belong to one Windows user: on a shared server each user only sees their own.
